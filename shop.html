Why Cozy Moments Matter

Our environments influence how we feel. Creating a calming space can remind your nervous system that it is safe to slow down and rest.

A cozy reset might be:

  • Lighting a candle before journaling
  • Taking five quiet minutes with your favorite drink
  • Creating a calming nighttime routine
  • Keeping reminders around you to pause and breathe
